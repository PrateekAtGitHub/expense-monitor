Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Your financial records are personal. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to record.
  • Technical information — basic logs such as browser type and request times, used to keep the service secure and working.

We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and category breakdowns.
  • Respond to support requests you send us.
  • Detect and prevent misuse of the service.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database operated for Spendly. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes and are never kept in plain text. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can edit or delete individual expenses at any time, and if you delete your account, your associated data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and basic browser information when a page loads. Apart from this, we do not share your personal or expense data with third parties, except where required by law.

If we introduce new third-party services in the future, we will update this policy before doing so.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, or want to access, correct, or delete your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
